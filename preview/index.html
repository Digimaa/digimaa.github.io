How it works

1

Parent builds a routine

Morning, bedtime, dinner, or a custom checklist. Make every step literal and clear — DigiMaa speaks them aloud.

2

Send it to your child’s phone

Tap Send to my child. Their phone opens in a simple child mode: greeting and Start — no editing screens.

3

They follow along, one step at a time

Each step is spoken. They press Done (or Pause / Resume when they need a break). Praise after every step keeps them moving.

Who it’s for

Kids who need clear, literal steps

Some children do best when instructions are one at a time and exactly what to do — not a long list shouted from the kitchen.

Busy parents

Set the routine once. DigiMaa repeats it calmly so you can focus on the next thing (or finish your coffee).

Anyone who likes structure

Custom routines work for after-school, chores, or getting out the door — not only mornings and bedtime.

DigiMaa is a family routine tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.

Why DigiMaa exists

DigiMaa was built by a parent for a child who needs clear, literal, one-step-at-a-time guidance. Long verbal lists were easy to miss. A calm voice saying the next step — then waiting for Done — worked better.

We made it for other families who want the same: structure without nagging, and wording you can tailor to how your child actually listens.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

Not yet. Routines are stored on the device. Family sync and sign-in are on the roadmap.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone (and your child’s phone after you send a routine). DigiMaa does not require an account or upload your routines to a DigiMaa server today.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. Open digimaa.app in Safari, then use Share → Add to Home Screen for an app-like icon. Android browsers work too.

What does my child see?

A greeting and big Start buttons for their routines — not the parent editing screens. You unlock parent tools with a short gate on their device if needed.

Is DigiMaa a medical or therapy product?

No. DigiMaa is a practical routine checklist with spoken steps for families. It does not provide medical advice.

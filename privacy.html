Privacy Policy

DRAFT – have this reviewed by a lawyer before publishing. Last updated: November 20, 2026.

DigiMaa (“the App”) is published by Net Zero Strategic, LLC (“we”, “us”).

What we collect

We do not require an account and we do not collect names, voice recordings, or routines on our servers. Routines, the optional child’s name, language and settings are stored only on your device.

Microphone and speech

Voice mode is optional. When enabled, the device’s built-in speech recognition (Apple or Google) converts speech to text to detect phrases like “I am done”. Audio may be processed by Apple or Google under their privacy policies. We do not store or receive audio or transcripts. Touch mode never uses the microphone.

Location

The optional weather reminder asks for approximate location and sends rounded coordinates to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to get the current temperature, and to BigDataCloud (bigdatacloud.com) to find the country so temperatures are spoken in °C or °F. Only the country code is kept on the device. We do not store your location. You can turn this off in the App.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check subscription status; it receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts, not payment card details.

Crash reports (optional, off by default)

To find and fix bugs, a parent can turn on “Send anonymous crash reports” in the App’s parent settings on each device. It is off unless a parent turns it on, can be turned off at any time, and is never shown to or changed from the child screens. When it is on and the App hits an error, the App sends one short report containing only: the type of error, a shortened error message with all user-entered text, numbers, links and non-code text removed, the names of the App’s own code files and line numbers where the error happened, the App version, the platform (web, iOS or Android) and the App language. A report never contains names (including a child’s name), routines or steps, voice, audio or transcripts, location, contacts, photos, advertising or device identifiers, user or install IDs, cookies, the page address, or the browser’s user‑agent string. Each report has a random number that is used only to avoid duplicates and is not stored on the device. At most five reports are sent per session.

Reports are processed for us by our error‑monitoring service provider (Sentry, Functional Software, Inc., EU data region) under a data processing agreement, only to keep the App working. As with any internet request, the provider’s servers see the device’s IP address and the standard information every browser sends with a request (such as browser type and version) in order to receive the report; we configure the service not to store IP addresses, and the provider may record only the general browser and operating-system type and version. Reports are kept for no longer than 90 days and are never used for advertising, profiling, or to contact anyone. Because reports contain no identifiers, we cannot link a report to a person or device. Until we finish setting up this service the setting is not shown and nothing is sent. App Store and Google Play builds do not send these reports; instead Apple and Google may share anonymous crash statistics with us if you allowed that in your phone’s settings (“Share with App Developers” on iPhone, “Usage & diagnostics” on Android), under their own privacy policies.

Website availability checks

We run an automatic check every few minutes that loads our public website from a server to confirm it is online. It does not involve any user, device or personal data.

Children

The App is intended to be set up by a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, show ads, sell or share personal information, build profiles, or use third‑party analytics, advertising or tracking. Optional crash reports (above) are anonymous, are used only to support the internal operation of the App (maintaining and analysing its functioning), and are turned on only by a parent.

Legal basis and your choices

EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland: optional crash reports are sent only with the consent of the parent or guardian, given by turning the setting on (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 8; ePrivacy rules on access to device information). You can withdraw consent at any time by turning the setting off. India: crash reports are processed only with the parent’s consent and never for tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising. Brazil: processing is limited to what is necessary, in the child’s best interest, with a parent’s specific consent (LGPD Art. 14). California and other US states: we do not sell or share personal information. Wherever you live you may contact us to ask about, access, correct or delete data, or to complain; you may also complain to your local data protection authority. Because we hold no identifiers, we will usually have no data that relates to you.

Privacy Policy

DRAFT – have this reviewed by a lawyer before publishing. Last updated: November 20, 2026.

DigiMaa (“the App”) is published by Net Zero Strategic, LLC (“we”, “us”).

What we collect

We do not require an account and we do not collect names, voice recordings, or routines on our servers. Routines, the optional child’s name, language and settings are stored only on your device.

Microphone and speech

Voice mode is optional. When enabled, the device’s built-in speech recognition (Apple or Google) converts speech to text to detect phrases like “I am done”. Audio may be processed by Apple or Google under their privacy policies. We do not store or receive audio or transcripts. Touch mode never uses the microphone.

Location and weather

The App never uses GPS or asks for location permission. For the optional jacket/weather reminder, a parent can type a city name once in the parent settings. The App sends that city name to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to look it up, and then asks Open-Meteo for today’s forecast (temperature and chance of rain) at that city’s map coordinates. Nothing else is sent. The city is stored on the device (and inside a routine link you choose to send to your child’s device), and you can clear it at any time by emptying the field. Like any internet request, Open-Meteo’s servers see the device’s IP address to answer; see open-meteo.com/en/terms for their privacy terms.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check subscription status; it receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts, not payment card details.

Crash reports

The website and the App contain no crash‑reporting, analytics or tracking code and send no error reports to us or anyone else. App Store and Google Play builds rely only on the crash statistics Apple and Google collect themselves when you allow it in your phone’s settings (“Share with App Developers” on iPhone, “Usage & diagnostics” on Android), under their own privacy policies.

Website availability checks

We may run an automatic check every few minutes that loads our public website from a server to confirm it is online. It does not involve any user, device or personal data.

Children

The App is intended to be set up by a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, show ads, sell or share personal information, build profiles, or use third‑party analytics, advertising or tracking.

Your choices and rights

The only information that leaves the device for these features is the city name you type (and that city’s coordinates) for the weather reminder; you can turn the reminder off or clear the city at any time. We do not sell or share personal information. Wherever you live (including the EU/EEA, UK, India, Brazil and California) you may contact us to ask about, access, correct or delete data, or to complain, and you may complain to your local data protection authority. Because we do not run servers that receive your data, we will usually hold no data that relates to you.
